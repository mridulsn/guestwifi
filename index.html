    About guestwifi

    guestwifi turns your WiFi name and password into a QR code that phones understand. Guests open their camera, tap the WiFi prompt and they're connected. It works with the camera app on iPhones and on most Android phones (or Google Lens).

    Choose a card: a small one for the fridge, a bigger one for a frame, or a table tent you fold in half. Print it, or save it as an image to send.

    If you change your WiFi password, make a new card.
